# Bundled visual assets

`countries.geojson`: Natural Earth 1:110m admin-0 country geometry, names, and cartographic label locations (public domain). Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson . Small states may be omitted at this scale; Singapore is supplemented by a location marker. Labels use the source dataset's cartographic conventions. Coordinates shown in the UI are label locations, not capitals or radio positions.

`land.geojson`: Natural Earth 1:110m land polygons, public-domain map data.
Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson
Natural Earth terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

The globe texture, atmosphere, stars, terrain mesh, and radio markers are generated locally. The terrain remains a schematic illustration, not a real elevation dataset or an RF model.
